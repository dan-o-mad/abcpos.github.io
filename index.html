Accounting, not reporting

Your books write themselves.

Most tills hand you a sales report and leave the accounting to someone else. Every sale here posts a real double-entry: cash to the drawer, VAT to what you owe, cost against stock.

Which means the trial balance on the right was not typed by anybody. It is just what the till did today.
